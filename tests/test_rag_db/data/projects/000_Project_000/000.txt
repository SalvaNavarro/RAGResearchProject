
Dezeen Agenda features Foster + Partners' plans
for "London's lowest whole-life carbon high-rise"

The latest edition of our weekly Dezeen Agenda newsletter features the approval of Foster +
Partners' 18 Blackfriars Road development in London. Subscribe to Dezeen Agenda now. UK
architecture studio Foster + Partners has been granted planning approval for 18 Blackfriars Road, a
development that will contain two residential blocks and a 45-storey office skyscraper. The studio
claimed that the project would have the lowest carbon footprint of any tall building in London. "[18
Blackfriars Road] will feature London's lowest whole-life carbon high-rise, which also aims to be the
first to achieve the WELL Community Gold rating," said Foster + Partners head of studio Nigel
