
Gather outdoor seating by Foster + Partners
Industrial Design for Escofet

Dezeen Showroom: Foster + Partners Industrial Design aimed to create relaxed outdoor areas with
Gather, its ultra-thin concrete seating solution for Spanish street furniture brand Escofet. Gather
outdoor seating is a modular system made from Escofet's own ultra-high-performance concrete,
called Slimconcrete, which is finished by hand. The Gather seating collection is made from slimline
concreteUsing Slimconcrete enabled Foster + Partners Industrial Design to achieve a refined and
slender profile with the furniture, which tapers to a 40-millimetre thickness at the edge of its
backrest and cantilevered seat. The Gather system includes linear, concave, convex and end
modules, which can be combined to create configurations ranging from enclosed pocket spaces to
long lines of curving or straight seating. The seating solution promotes the use of public spaceAlong
with another new Foster + Partners Industrial Design collection, titled Tiers, Gather was developed
in tandem with the architecture practice's landscape and urban design teams, intended to
encourage the active use of public space. "With their understated elegance and refined profiles,
Tiers and Gather provide an array of creative options for architects, urban planners and landscape
designers," said Foster + Partners head of industrial design Mike Holland. The images are by
